Privacy Policy
Last updated: 6 August 2026

Summary

Chroma Shift is developed by Tsereq Ya Vun. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or location. Your game progress is stored on your own device.

Three things do leave your device: anonymous usage and crash statistics that tell us how the game is performing, and — only if you choose to set a nickname — your leaderboard entry. The game also shows ads, and our advertising partner (Google AdMob) collects certain device and usage data in order to deliver those ads. All of this is explained in detail below.

1. Information stored on your device

Chroma Shift saves the following locally on your device so the game works between sessions. Unless you join the leaderboard (section 5), none of it is uploaded to us:

  • Level progress and highest level reached
  • Score, high score, and gameplay statistics (walls broken, play time)
  • In-game currency (Photons), owned items, unlocked cosmetics and themes
  • Daily quests, achievements, and daily login streak progress
  • Settings such as music volume, sound effects, haptics, and chosen language
  • A nickname and profile icon, if you choose to set one
  • A randomly generated identifier used to keep your progress consistent on this device

This information is removed when you uninstall the app or clear the app's data from your device settings.

One exception: if you choose a nickname in order to appear on the global leaderboard, your nickname, profile icon and scores are also uploaded and made public. That is explained in section 5, and it only ever happens if you set a nickname.

2. Advertising

Chroma Shift displays advertising provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process data such as:

  • Your device's advertising identifier
  • Device information (model, operating system version, language, general region)
  • IP address, which may be used to infer approximate (non-precise) location
  • Ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown, viewed, or clicked

This processing is carried out by Google, not by us. To learn how Google uses this data, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you are shown a consent screen before personalised ads are used. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Ad Privacy Settings inside the game.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, from your device's system settings (usually under Settings → Privacy → Ads).

4. Analytics and crash reporting

Chroma Shift uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how the game is played and to find bugs. This is anonymous and statistical — it tells us things like “how many players finished level 4”, never who you are. The data processed includes:

  • Gameplay events: levels started, completed or failed, stars earned, ads watched
  • Aggregate profile signals: highest level reached, an approximate skill bracket
  • Device model, operating system version, language and country
  • An app-instance identifier generated by Firebase, which resets if you reinstall the app or clear its data
  • Crash reports: the error, the level it happened on, and technical device state at the time

We do not receive your name, email address, phone number, contacts or precise location through this. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the consent choice you make in the privacy prompt when you first open the game is applied to this data too.

Firebase is operated by Google. See Firebase’s privacy information and Google’s Privacy Policy.

5. Leaderboard (optional)

The global leaderboard is opt-in. Nothing about you is published unless you choose a nickname. If you do, the following is uploaded to our Google Firestore database and is publicly visible to other players:

  • The nickname you typed and the profile icon you picked
  • Your high score, weekly and monthly best scores, total walls broken and highest level
  • The random device identifier described in section 1, used as your row’s key
  • An anonymous Firebase sign-in identifier. The game signs in to Google Firebase anonymously the first time you submit a score — this creates no account and asks for no email or password. It exists only so that nobody else can overwrite or delete your leaderboard entry.

Please do not use your real name or any personal information as your nickname. If you want your leaderboard entry removed, email us at the address in section 11 and we will delete it.

6. Permissions the app uses

  • Internet access — required to load ads, send anonymous analytics and crash reports, and show the leaderboard.
  • Vibration — used for haptic feedback during gameplay. Can be turned off in Settings.
  • Notifications — used only to remind you about your daily reward or login streak. You may deny or revoke this permission at any time; the game works normally without it.

7. Children

Chroma Shift is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the address below and we will take appropriate action.

8. Data retention and deletion

Your game data is stored on your device, so you can delete all of it at any time by uninstalling Chroma Shift or by clearing the app's data from your device settings. Doing so also resets the analytics app-instance identifier described in section 4.

A leaderboard entry, if you created one, stays in our database until you ask us to remove it. Email the address in section 11 and we will delete it. Analytics data is retained by Google for up to 14 months; crash reports are retained for up to 90 days. Data held by Google in connection with advertising is governed by Google's own retention policies, linked in section 2.

9. Where your data goes, and your rights

International transfers

Advertising, analytics and crash reporting are provided by Google and are processed on Google’s infrastructure, which may be located outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. The leaderboard database is hosted in the European Union. Where data leaves the EEA, Google relies on the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses; details are in Google’s privacy resources.

Why we are allowed to process it

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we rely on your consent for personalised advertising and for analytics — that is the choice you make in the prompt when you first open the game, which you can change at any time from Settings. We rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the game working for crash reporting and for protecting the leaderboard from abuse. Your leaderboard entry is published on the basis of your consent, given by choosing a nickname.

Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of the data we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, or to stop using it. In practice almost everything is already in your hands: uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes your local save and resets your analytics identifier, and we will delete your leaderboard entry on request. Email us at the address in section 11 and we will respond within 30 days.

If you are in the EEA or the UK and you are not satisfied with how we have handled your data, you have the right to complain to your national data protection authority.

Security

All data sent between the game and our providers travels over encrypted HTTPS connections. Leaderboard entries can only be written by the device that created them.
