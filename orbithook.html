Privacy Policy
Last updated: 3 November 2026

Summary

Orbit Hook is developed by Tsereq Ya Vun. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or location. Your game progress is stored on your own device and is never uploaded to us.

Two things leave your device: ads are served by Google AdMob, which collects certain device and usage data to deliver and measure them, and the game sends anonymous usage statistics (such as which levels are played and finished) to Google Firebase Analytics so that we can fix levels that are too hard. Both are explained below, and both respect your consent choice.

No account needed No name or email collected Progress stored on device Anonymous analytics Ads by Google AdMob No in-app purchases

1. Information stored on your device

Orbit Hook saves the following locally so the game works between sessions. None of it is uploaded to us:

  • Level progress, stars earned and best times
  • Free Flight records and daily run results
  • In-game currencies (stardust and crystals), powers and unlocked cosmetics
  • Daily quests, achievements and daily login streak progress
  • Settings such as sound, music, haptics, chosen language and ad-consent choices
  • Basic technical counters used to keep the game consistent (for example, how many days since install)

This information is removed when you uninstall the app or clear the app's data from your device settings.

2. Advertising

Orbit Hook is free and supported by advertising provided by Google AdMob. The game shows full-screen ads at natural breaks (for example after finishing a level) and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch in exchange for a bonus. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process data such as:

  • Your device's advertising identifier
  • Device information (model, operating system version, language, general region)
  • IP address, which may be used to infer approximate (non-precise) location
  • Ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown, viewed, or clicked
  • Diagnostic data about how the ads software performed

This processing is carried out by Google, not by us. To learn how Google uses this data, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you are shown a consent screen (Google's User Messaging Platform) before personalised ads are used. Where the rules require it, you can review or change your choice at any time with the privacy button in the game's Settings.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, from your device's system settings (usually under Settings → Privacy → Ads).

4. Analytics

Orbit Hook uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is played — for example how many players finish level 4, or where they stop playing — so that we can balance the levels. It is statistical and anonymous: it never tells us who you are. The data includes:

  • Gameplay events: levels started, completed, failed or left, ads watched, rewards claimed, items bought with in-game currency, achievements
  • Aggregate signals: highest level reached, days since install and the language you chose
  • Device model, operating system version, language and country
  • An app-instance identifier generated by Firebase, which resets when you reinstall the app or clear its data

We do not receive your name, email address, phone number, contacts or precise location this way. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland analytics is sent only after you agree in the consent screen. Everywhere, you can switch it off at any time with the Share usage data switch in the game's Settings; nothing is sent while it is off.

Firebase is operated by Google. See Firebase's privacy information and Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Permissions the app uses

  • Internet access and network state — required to load and show ads and to send anonymous analytics.
  • Advertising ID — used by Google AdMob to deliver and measure ads (see section 2) and by Firebase Analytics (see section 4). You can reset or delete it in your device settings.
  • Vibration — used for haptic feedback during gameplay. Can be turned off in Settings.

The game does not ask for access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

6. Children

Orbit Hook is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the address below and we will take appropriate action.

7. Data retention and deletion

Your game data is stored on your device, so you can delete all of it at any time by uninstalling Orbit Hook or by clearing the app's data from your device settings. We hold no copy of it. Analytics data is retained by Google for up to 14 months. Data that Google holds in connection with advertising is governed by Google's own retention policies, linked in section 2.

8. Where your data goes, and your rights

Advertising and analytics are provided by Google and processed on Google's infrastructure, which may be located outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Where data leaves the EEA, Google relies on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses; details are in Google's privacy resources.

If you are in the EEA or the UK, we rely on your consent for personalised advertising and for analytics — that is the choice you make in the consent screen, which you can change later. You can ask us for a copy of the data we hold about you (which is none), or ask us to delete or correct it; email the address in section 10 and we will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with how your data has been handled, you have the right to complain to your national data protection authority.

All data sent between the game and Google's advertising and analytics servers travels over encrypted HTTPS connections.
